About LearnHub

On a mission to make world-class education accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Story

Built by Learners, for Learners

LearnHub was founded in 2020 by a group of engineers and educators who were frustrated with the gap between traditional education and the skills the modern world actually needs. We set out to build a platform where world-class instructors could reach anyone with an internet connection.

Today, we serve 50,000+ students across 120+ countries with courses that are project-based, up-to-date, and taught by practitioners — not just academics.
