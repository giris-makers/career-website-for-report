About Nexus

We're on a mission to connect people with opportunities that matter.

Our Mission

At Nexus, we believe the right career opportunity can change everything. We build tools that help people find meaningful work and help companies find outstanding talent. Every day, we work to make that connection faster, fairer, and more human.

Our Culture

We are a team of curious, driven people who care deeply about our work and each other. We move fast, but never sacrifice quality. We disagree openly and decide together. We celebrate wins and learn from setbacks. Above all, we treat everyone — teammates, customers, and candidates — with respect.

  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage
  • Flexible remote and hybrid work options
  • Generous PTO and company-wide recharge days
  • $2,000 annual learning & development budget
  • Home office stipend
  • 401(k) with company match
  • Parental leave for all caregivers
